# Literary quote seed sources

The two initial quotes are short Spanish excerpts from Miguel de Cervantes's
*Don Quijote de la Mancha*, second part (1615). They are general remarks about
reading, unrelated to the club's current reading. No modern translation,
editorial notes or substantive plot excerpts are copied. The original work is
public domain; the seed does not imply verification of arbitrary administrator
submissions. Review attribution and rights before adding further corpus entries.

- II, chapter 3: the bachiller's remark about books, verified against the

  The short quotation omits the narrative speaker interruption.
- II, chapter 25: Don Quijote's remark about reading and travelling, verified
  against the [Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes published text](https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/obra-visor/el-ingenioso-hidalgo-don-quijote-de-la-mancha--0/html/fef04e52-82b1-11df-acc7-002185ce6064_83.html).

  excerpt's initial word. Attribution is to the author/work, not a claim that he
  originated the underlying proverb.
